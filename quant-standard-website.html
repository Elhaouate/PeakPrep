FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

Do you teach SAT Verbal, GMAT Verbal, or GRE Verbal too?

No — this is a deliberate specialization in the quantitative sections only. That focus allows for deeper, faster progress than a generalist tutor covering every section.

How many sessions will I actually need?

It depends on your starting point and target score: roughly 10–30 hours for SAT Math, 10–30 hours for GMAT Quant & Data Insights, and 8–22 hours for GRE Quant. Your diagnostic session will narrow this down.

Is the SAT still on paper?

No. The SAT has been fully digital and adaptive since 2024, and that's the exact format used in every session — including the on-screen calculator built into the test.

What's the difference between GMAT Quant and Data Insights?

Quantitative Reasoning tests traditional problem-solving with numbers and algebra. Data Insights covers multi-source reasoning, table analysis, and interpreting data in charts or graphics.

Can I mix online and in-person sessions?

Yes. Many students split their package between in-person sessions when convenient and online the rest of the time — materials and approach stay identical either way.

How does billing work?

Packages are paid upfront and valid for 2–3 months from purchase. Single sessions are billed and paid per session if you'd rather not commit to a package yet.
